How we approach the work

One conversation.
A clearer plan.

Forge Fire is your point of contact for coordinating the project. Specialist subcontractors handle the trade work; we focus on scope, scheduling, communication and quality coordination.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Share the location, intended use, approximate size and timing. Photos or sketches can help explain your idea.

  2. Define what’s included

    Review site conditions, project requirements and subcontractor input. Agree what belongs in the scope and what stays outside it.

  3. Review the next step

    Discuss a scoped estimate and proposed sequence before committing to work. Changes need review too.

Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

Can I get a price before sharing project details?

We need to understand the location, intended use, size, site conditions and included scope first. Your initial inquiry starts that conversation; it is not an instant quote or a commitment to build.

Does Forge Fire do the trade work?

Our model centers on coordinating customers and specialist subcontractors. We organize the scope, schedule and quality coordination, with delivery arrangements discussed for each project.

Can I still request plasma cutting?

Yes. Use the Metal & CNC section for a plasma inquiry or the existing design tool. Partner capabilities, material availability and project requirements need confirmation before quoting.
